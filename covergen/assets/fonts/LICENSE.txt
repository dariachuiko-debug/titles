DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf is part of the DejaVu Fonts project (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/),
derived from Bitstream Vera. Released under a permissive, redistributable license
(Bitstream Vera License + public domain additions) that allows bundling and modification.
Chosen here for its full Cyrillic coverage, so poster titles render identically on every
machine that runs this pipeline, regardless of which fonts happen to be installed locally.
